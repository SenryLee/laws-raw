# 科技部国际合作司关于中欧煤炭利用近零排放发电技术合作征集预可行性研究项目的通知

根据我部与欧盟委员会2006年2月签订的《关于通过二氧化碳捕获与封存实现近零排放发电技术的合作谅解备忘录》，中欧双方启动了煤炭利用近零排放发电技术合作项目（以下简称“中欧NZEC合作项目”）。2006-2009年，中欧NZEC合作项目开展了第一阶段合作，涵盖了能源消费预测、捕集技术经济性评价、我国部分地区二氧化碳地质封存潜力评估以及知识共享等活动。

根据我部与欧盟委员会2009年11月签订的《关于通过二氧化碳捕集与封存实现煤炭利用近零排放发电技术第II阶段合作的谅解备忘录》，并基于第一阶段的成功合作，中欧双方进一步开展以推动实施碳捕获与封存的全流程示范工程为主要目标的第二阶段合作（NZEC-II），并将NZEC-II分为两期：NZEC-IIA和NZEC-IIB。IIA的中心工作是对国内的候选项目进行比较研究，遴选3个项目开展预可行性研究，并进一步选出1个项目作为IIB的支持对象；NZEC-IIB的中心工作是通过对选出项目的资金与技术支持，协助企业完成工程详细勘察、可行性研究及工程设计。

根据中欧NZEC-IIA阶段合作的执行协议，启动中欧NZEC-IIA技术示范预可行性研究项目的征集工作：

**一、**征集时间自2013年6月6日到2013年7月22日。

**二、**根据专家评审结果，我部将选择3个项目，经中欧NZEC合作项目联合指导委员会通过立项。

**三、**3个项目的执行期为2013年9月1日至2014年5月31日。

**四、**根据项目执行情况，经专家评审及中欧NZEC合作项目联合指导委员会确定，将从3个NZEC-IIA项目中遴选出一项作为NZEC-IIB项目进一步实施。

预可行性研究项目申请说明

**一、**总原则

- 项目征集的目的是促进碳捕集、利用与封存技术在我国的发展及推广，加强与欧盟的技术交流与合作。

- 项目征集在技术路线选择上没有特殊要求和倾向性。考虑到煤基碳捕集与封存技术尚不成熟，项目征集将解决一系列技术难题作为目标，以促进其商业化。

- 二氧化碳利用作为增加收益和降低成本的重要手段应在项目建议书中优先考虑。

**二、**申报对象条件

候选项目应符合我国碳捕集、利用与封存技术发展的整体战略，征集对象主要针对计划在近年内开展全流程CCUS技术示范并长期运行的中国境内企业。所申请项目须满足以下条件：

- 具有包含二氧化碳的捕集、输送和封存（或利用）的全流程技术系统；

- 具有每年二氧化碳封存或利用量30万吨以上的规模；技术上具备85%二氧化碳捕集率的能力；优先考虑采用下游热利用。

- 具备5年内开始实施的工程条件；其中1年预可研，2年可行性研究和工程设计，2年建设。

- 有建成后长期运行的计划；

- 承诺发表项目审查所需的非知识产权数据和实物资料；

- 承诺发表在项目执行过程中产生的非知识产权信息；

- 项目申请者应为企业法人，若涉及多个企业，须联合提交申请。允许中欧企业联合申请该项目。

- 申请人需提供项目所需经费的依据，包括投资预评估和运行成本和融资渠道。

- 承诺开展公众接受度和封存地情况调查的相关研究，并公开结果。

**三、**项目遴选标准

对于满足以上资格条件的申请项目，根据以下标准进行筛选。筛选条件设定的基本原则是找出具有示范工程实施前景较好的项目。

- 示范项目技术路线合理性：包括系统定义、关键问题识别、解决方案的合理性。

- 示范项目的成熟度：包括基础设施、工业设施等已具备的工程条件的完备度以及所需技术、场地与装备的可获取度。

- 项目的复杂度：包括涉及范围，投入规模，融资模式与计划，是否存在利用权纠纷，以及成本高低等；

- 项目的创新性：包括技术先进性、能耗及项目是否有符合我国国情的创新点等。

**四、**项目优先资助条件

对于具备良好工程实施条件的项目，将根据以下条件进行优先资助。优先资助条件的设定主要围绕预可研的条件。

- 预可研条件的完备度：包括封存场地条件的勘察程度、认识程度、物质样品及是否具备可利用钻孔等；

- 预可研的成熟度：包括前期工作基础及工作深入程度等；

- 预可研计划的可行性：研究思路、内容与进度是否合理并可行；

- 预可研预算的合理性。

**五、**经费支持

根据申请书和评审结果，每个选中项目将获得不超过27万欧元的经费开展预可研活动。预算包含预可研所需的实地考察、调研、数据收集。申请单位需提供财政配套。

Instructions for Application

1. General Principles：

The aim of the call is to promote the development of CCUS technology and its application in China， with enhanced technical exchange and cooperation with EU partners；

This Call is open to all the technology options so no pre-set， pre-defined or preferred technical path for the call has been declared. As coal based CCS technology is still not mature， an important objective of the project is to strengthen the development of a range of technical solutions that can be taken forward to commercial prototype demonstration in the near future；

Project applicants are encouraged to consider utilization of the captured CO2 in the proposed project， as means to generate additional revenue and reduce overall costs.

2. Eligibility of applications：

All proposals must meet the requirement to take forward projects that are compatible with China’s overall CCS development strategy. The plans to construct full-chain CCUS technology demonstration in coming years， shall comply with the following criteria：

The demonstration must include the whole technical chain of CO2 capture， transportation and storage /utilization；

The proposed projects must have the storage/utilization level larger than 300，000 tonnes CO2 per year， and be technically ready for treating 85% CO2 from the gas stream. Downstream heat recovery to improve the overall process efficiency would be encouraged.

It’s requested that the project shall be ready for implementation within five　years， this comprising one year for the pre-feasibility study， two years for detailed feasibility study and engineering design plus two years for construction；

The project owner must have a long-term operation plan for the plant with the CCUS integration when and after the project is completed；

Applicants shall make commitment to provide non-IPR data and materials to facilitate project evaluation；

Applicants must be committed to publish　non-IPR information and materials generated during the implementation of the project openly to public；

Applicants must be the legal persons of enterprises， and joint application is requested for projects involve more than one enterprises. Joint Chinese and EU/EEA applications will be allowed.

The tenders should provide a document for justifying the funding resources required. Applications should include a preliminary assessment of investment and operation costs and of sources of financing.

Application should include a commitment to design and implement a programme for public awareness and acceptance and also a research programme to investigate the performance of a storage site with results to be made publicly available.

3.　Assessment：

Criteria for project selection： all the qualified proposed projects shall be considered and reviewed equally in accordance with the following criteria， with an aim to identify the most promising demonstration projects.

Rationality of the technical path of the near-zero emissions coal fired power generation technology demonstration project： including system definition， identification of key problems and justification of solutions；

Maturity of the demonstration project： including readiness of the infrastructure and industrial facilities for project implementation， and availability of the necessary expertise， site and equipment；

Project complexity： including operational scope， scale of input， financing model and plan， IPR disputes， and operational cost； and

Project innovativeness： including technological sophistication， energy consumption， and compliance with the Chinese context-bound innovation.

4.The priority for project identification and financial support

The priority for project identification and financial support will be given to those projects with sound and feasible project implementation conditions to conduct the pre-feasibility study.

Preparedness： exploration and examination of the storage site， material sampling， and availability of drill hole；

Maturity： pre-project ground-forging work and the quality and achievement in the work；

Feasibility： reasonableness and feasibleness of the study approach， contents and progress； and budgetary rationality.

Innovation： leading technology， lower energy consumption， novelty and suitability for implementation in China.

5. Support for the Identified Projects：

Based on the proposal and the evaluation results， up to 270，000 Euro will be provided to each of the three identified projects to conduct the pre-feasibility study. The budget shall cover field test， survey and acquiring of necessary data for the pre-feasibility study. The project entity or entities shall make their own financial contribution to this study as necessary.
